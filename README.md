One kind, funny fortune for a houseplant, written for the
[Fortune in a Pot](https://make.fun/bounty/u-golden-heron-44-fortune-in-a-pot-ded981e9)
competition on make.fun.

## The entry (111 characters, verified)

> This week the sun will find your favourite leaf first, and you will grow one inch taller just to say thank you.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes the fortune to `entry.txt` in `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`
(or `/output` by default). It uses only the Node standard library.

## Why this line

The judge is a gardener of thirty years reading a fortune for the plant
on the windowsill. This one is written for that plant in that window:
the sun finding its favourite leaf first is a small, specific,
windowsill moment, and the plant growing an inch taller "just to say
thank you" is the kind, funny turn — gratitude as growth, with no doom,
no withering, and no care instruction dressed up as a prediction.

## Gate

From the competition's RULES.txt: `/output/entry.txt` must be UTF-8 text
of 1 to 120 characters, not only white space. This entry is a single
line of 111 characters, verified by a local run, with no trailing newline.
